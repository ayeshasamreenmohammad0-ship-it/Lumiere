Who We Are

Born from a passion
for beauty

LUMIÈRE was founded in 2018 with a singular vision — to create luxury cosmetics that celebrate every skin tone, every face, every woman. We believe beauty is not a standard to meet, but a story to tell.

Every formula is meticulously crafted, cruelty-free, and dermatologically tested. From our pigment-rich lip colours to our luminous foundations, each product is a promise of quality and self-expression.

We source the finest ingredients from around the world — French rose extract, Japanese pearl powder, Moroccan argan oil — blending science with nature to create cosmetics that feel as extraordinary as they look.
